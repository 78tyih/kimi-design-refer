# PropFirm.TV — 12 Family Benchmark Prompts

这份文件用于复现 `kimi-design-refer` 的 12 个视觉家族 Benchmark。

所有 Prompt 使用同一份 PropFirm.TV 内容，只改变视觉 Family。这样可以更清晰地比较 Family 本身的差异。

## Shared Brief

```text
Brand: propfirm.tv
Subtitle: Prop Firm Intelligence
Chinese line: 规则追踪 · 出金资讯 · 平台对比 · 市场动态
Tagline: Track. Compare. Decide.
Medium: poster-social
Aspect ratio: 4:5
```

> 建议：如果 Agent 支持先规划再生成，让它先确认 Family fidelity、主视觉事件、负空间比例和 Avoid List，再开始生成。

## 01 — `black-orbit`

```text
Use $kimi-design-refer.

Mode: Generate
Medium: poster-social
Family: black-orbit
Aspect ratio: 4:5

Brand: propfirm.tv
Subtitle: Prop Firm Intelligence
Chinese line: 规则追踪 · 出金资讯 · 平台对比 · 市场动态
Tagline: Track. Compare. Decide.

Create a premium PropFirm.TV campaign poster using the black-orbit family.

Use a nearly black granular field with approximately 55–75% quiet negative space.
Build one dominant orbital intelligence system using oversized cropped circular membranes,
thin white contour lines, elastic intersections, sparse nodes and trajectory marks.
The system should feel like market intelligence infrastructure rather than decorative space art.

Use tiny cobalt-blue anomaly sparks or nodes occupying less than 2% of the frame.
Keep typography clean, modern and restrained.
Add only a small evidence/index layer for Rule Changes, Payout News,
Firm Comparison and Trader Research.

The eye should land on one primary orbital cluster.
The visual should communicate signal routing, structure and intelligence.

Avoid:
- neon cyberpunk;
- full-frame blue/purple gradients;
- glassmorphism;
- generic glowing node networks;
- multiple equal focal points;
- official Kimi / Moonshot branding.
```
