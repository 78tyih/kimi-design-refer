# Third-Party Notices

Kimi Design Refer is an original, unofficial visual reference system.

It was informed by high-level design-system ideas from two public MIT-licensed projects:

- `Fannie0715/kimi-inspired-poster`
- `LiamGvchi/gc-minimal-zine-poster`

The latter contributes inspiration around extreme negative space, one small visual event, sparse editorial typography, one chromatic anchor, and print/scan reproduction. These mechanics are reorganized here into five original Zine Editorial Extension families designed to interoperate with the Kimi-inspired core system.

No source example image, exact composition, Kimi/Moonshot logo, or proprietary campaign asset is included or claimed as original work of this repository.
