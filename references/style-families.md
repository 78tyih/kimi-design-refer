# Style Families

Choose exactly one primary family.

## Kimi Core Families

### 1. black-orbit
Dark granular field, huge thin circular membranes, elastic intersections, sparse junction sparks. Best for networks, agent systems, collaboration, ecosystems. Use modern neutral sans + tiny mono. Avoid neon rings, starfields, generic node-link diagrams, blue-purple gradients.

### 2. pixel-moon
Near-black low-bit universe, coarse ordered dither, pixel moon/portal/specimen, bitmap interface cues. Best for developer culture, hiring, exploration, moonshot/build themes. Avoid vaporwave, cute game characters, smooth gradients.

### 3. white-lab
Cool pale laboratory field, instruments, hands, calibrated geometry, technical plates, contour/mesh systems. Best for research, reasoning, architecture, tools, human-AI calibration. Avoid cozy desk photography and fake-formula clutter.

### 4. cosmic-index
Clean light field, indexed intelligence, specimen + diagram + glyph/ASCII/astronomical cues. Best for model releases, memory, scale, knowledge, research summaries. Use editorial serif + mono indices. Avoid generic outer-space art and vintage astronomy cosplay.

### 5. gray-proofboard
Medium-gray or black editorial field with one dominant proof fragment, oversized index, strict invisible grid, long unused intervals. Best for launches, capability proof, cases, reports. Avoid dashboards, equal card columns and scrapbook boards.

### 6. kinetic-glyph
One symbol or word transforms across material/computational states. Best for slogans, version reveals, language, multimodality and identity teasers. The glyph is the hero. Avoid random font collage and quote-card design.

### 7. simulation-window
One vivid generated/simulated scene contained inside a monochrome evidence shell. Best for 3D, spatial generation, interactive prototypes, games and multimodal outputs. Avoid full-bleed fantasy art, multiple saturated scenes and glossy chrome.

## Optional Zine Editorial Extension

The seven families above remain the Kimi Core Families. When the user asks for paper texture, poetic editorial, indie-zine, archive-note, or a Kimi × Zine blend, also read `references/zine-editorial-extension.md`.

Five distilled extension families are callable:

8. `zine/micro-archive`
9. `zine/dual-memory`
10. `zine/color-cutout`
11. `zine/type-relic`
12. `zine/orbit-notes`

These five are distilled from a source system with one parent zine style and ten layout families. A `zine/*` family may be the primary family for poster/editorial work or a secondary overlay paired with exactly one Kimi Core Family.

## Fidelity rule

A family is recognized by its system behavior, not surface color alone. Do not mix multiple Kimi Core Families and multiple Zine Families in one composition.
