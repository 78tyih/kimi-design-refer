# Philosophy

Kimi Design Refer treats visual design as evidence of intelligence at work rather than spectacle about AI.

Core equation:

`quiet field + bounded evidence system + technical hierarchy + visible process + restrained anomaly`

Principles:
- reduce a brief to one visual thesis;
- use negative space as structure, not emptiness;
- concentrate complexity inside one bounded region;
- show a process such as connect, index, measure, calibrate, infer, transform or simulate;
- prefer evidence, specimens, diagrams and transformations over generic futuristic decoration;
- keep anomalies small and intentional;
- maintain originality: learn grammar, never copy a known campaign frame.

When the Zine Editorial Extension is active, the same philosophy becomes more tactile and intimate: one small visual event, one chromatic anchor, sparse editorial type, and coherent print/scan texture.
